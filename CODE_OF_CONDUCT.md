# Code of Conduct — ZAIRE Backend

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of the ZAIRE backend pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to building software that is excellent, secure, and respectful of the people who use and contribute to it.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behaviour

- Use welcoming, inclusive, and technically precise language
- Respect differing viewpoints and implementation approaches
- Provide constructive feedback focused on code and ideas, not people
- Accept feedback gracefully and iterate
- Prioritise the long-term health and security of the project
- Give credit where it is due

### Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments in any form
- Publishing private information (email addresses, API keys, etc.) without consent
- Personal attacks or arguments unrelated to the project
- Spam or self-promotional content unrelated to ZAIRE
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting
